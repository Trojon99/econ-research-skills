# Humanizing Economics Prose

## Purpose

Use this reference for completed English economics prose that is generic, formulaic, over-polished, or described by the user as AI-written. Replace generic fluency with specific economic reasoning. Keep the result restrained, journal-appropriate, and accountable to the evidence; do not make it conversational merely to create personality.

Treat an AI-flavor assessment as a diagnosis of textual patterns, not proof of who wrote the passage. Never report a probability of AI authorship.

## Preserve hard constraints

- Preserve facts, estimates, signs, magnitudes, uncertainty, samples, institutional details, mechanisms, assumptions, and identification boundaries.
- Preserve citations, citation keys, equations, notation, labels, references, footnotes, table and figure numbers, and LaTeX environments unless the user requests changes to them.
- Keep causal verbs no stronger than the design warrants.
- Retain technical terms, repeated defined objects, passive constructions, and analytical adverbs when they serve a real function.
- Weaken, mark, or query an unsupported claim; never fill the gap with an invented fact, citation, mechanism, or robustness result.

## Diagnose patterns in clusters

Do not rewrite a passage solely because it contains formal vocabulary, polished grammar, passive voice, an author-year citation, or one familiar transition. Look for several reinforcing patterns and repair the underlying economic imprecision.

| Pattern | Repair |
|---|---|
| Inflated importance or promotional novelty | Name the question, setting, data advantage, identifying variation, model object, mechanism, counterfactual, or policy margin that is actually new or useful. |
| Generic transitions or throat-clearing | State the logical move directly: the next threat, test, comparison, moment, mechanism, or limitation. |
| Participial tails and broad implication endings | Replace them with the estimate, margin of adjustment, distributional incidence, parameter, model implication, or welfare object they are meant to describe. |
| Formulaic contribution claims | Specify what earlier work identifies or omits and what the present evidence changes. Use only the author's supplied positioning. |
| Vague attribution or implied consensus | Use verified citations or narrow the claim. Mark a citation gap instead of inventing authority. |
| Mechanical contrasts, forced triples, or abstract lists | Retain only distinctions and list items that do analytical work; state the substantive claim without rhetorical staging. |
| Synonym cycling | Repeat the defined economic object consistently unless a real distinction requires a different term. |
| False agency | Use the appropriate actor or inference: households choose, firms respond, estimates suggest, patterns are consistent with, and models imply. |
| Business jargon, chatbot framing, revision narration, or slogans | Replace it with the paper's substantive claim. Keep revision language only in a response letter. |
| Uniform sentence and paragraph rhythm | Let information content determine length. Use a short result sentence when useful, retain longer technical sentences when necessary, and break paragraphs when the economic object changes. |

Do not use a blacklist mechanically. A familiar phrase may be justified; the relevant question is whether it hides an economic object, evidentiary step, or logical relationship.

## Calibrate by section

| Section | Humanization target |
|---|---|
| Abstract | Move quickly through the question, setting or data, design or model, main quantitative result, warranted interpretation, and concrete contribution. |
| Introduction | Sharpen the puzzle, identification or quantification challenge, approach, main results, and precise contribution without grant-style promotion. |
| Literature review | Organize around distinctions in questions, methods, settings, mechanisms, and identified objects rather than cataloguing papers. |
| Data | Replace praise for a dataset with its variables, coverage, frequency, linkage, construction, and measurement advantage. |
| Empirical strategy | State the estimating object, identifying variation, assumption, threats, and inference plainly; do not claim clean identification before explaining why. |
| Results | Report sign, magnitude, unit, comparison or baseline, statistical precision, and economic interpretation when supplied. Do not decorate an estimate or infer an untested mechanism. |
| Mechanisms and heterogeneity | Separate the observed pattern, its interpretation, and evidence for the channel. Use cautious language for suggestive evidence. |
| Structural analysis | Keep agents, choices, states, timing, primitives, moments, fit, counterfactual definitions, maintained assumptions, and welfare objects visible. |
| Conclusion | State what the paper establishes, what it does not establish, and why the relevant limitation or policy margin matters. Avoid generic calls for future research. |

## Apply a two-pass rewrite

1. Identify the section type and the economic function of each paragraph.
2. Mark formulaic patterns, unsupported claims, unclear actors, and places where evidence and interpretation are misaligned.
3. Produce a conservative rewrite that preserves content, notation, citations, paragraph function, and academic register.
4. Audit whether any sentence could be inserted into an unrelated economics paper without meaningful change. Replace such language with the specific claim, evidence, mechanism, assumption, or limitation available in the source.
5. Revise once more for remaining generic transitions, promotional language, false agency, rhetorical padding, and overly tidy rhythm.

Do not vary rhythm by inserting fragments, punchlines, or casual asides. Do not achieve directness by deleting assumptions, caveats, or technical detail.

## Provide diagnostics only when useful

When the user requests an AI-flavor score or structured diagnosis, rate these dimensions from 1 to 10, where 10 means the prose is strong on that dimension:

- directness;
- specificity and evidence discipline;
- analytical density;
- reader trust, with little hand-holding or meta-commentary;
- authorial judgment about evidence and limits;
- sentence and paragraph rhythm.

Report the dimension scores, weakest dimensions, and revision priorities. Treat the scores as an editorial profile, not an authorship test, and do not aggregate them into a probability. For ordinary rewrites, use the checks internally and omit scores unless they clarify a material problem.

## Select the response shape

- For a short passage, return the final rewrite and add notes only for weakened claims, missing support, or unresolved ambiguity.
- For a long or high-stakes section, return a concise diagnosis, the final rewrite, and any remaining caveats.
- For a diagnostic-only request, return the stylistic signals, weakest dimensions, and prioritized repairs; include a rewrite only when requested.
